How Dig Funding supports archaeology

1. Create a compelling project page

Archaeologists present their research goals, timelines, and budgets. They can upload galleries, field updates, and exclusive posts that unlock for backers at chosen tiers.

2. Offer exclusive rewards

Reward tiers can include virtual site tours, behind-the-scenes journals, early access to excavation reports, or live Q&A sessions for dedicated supporters.

3. Engage a global community

Enthusiasts follow their favorite digs, fund new discoveries, and receive regular updates on progress, findings, and field challenges directly from the archaeologists.

Frequently asked questions

How do archaeologists share exclusive content?

Project creators can publish tiered updates, upload downloadable field notes, or schedule private livestreams. Supporters unlock content automatically when they back at the specified reward level.

What types of rewards can be offered?

Rewards range from public thank-you notes and postcards to custom 3D models, limited-edition prints, or opportunities to join a dig day virtually. Each tier highlights what supporters receive in return for their contribution.

How can I track project progress?

Follow projects to receive email updates and notifications whenever new field notes, discoveries, or exclusive content posts go live.
